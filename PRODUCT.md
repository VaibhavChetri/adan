# Adan Corporate

International corporate advisory firm. Corporate finance, M&A, strategy, risk
and coaching for mid-market firms trading across borders. UK-registered.

**Register:** brand. The site is marketing — design is the product.

**Audience:** owners and boards of mid-market firms contemplating a raise, a
sale, an acquisition or a cross-border move; and PE/VC funds and family offices.
They are financially literate, sceptical, and checking whether the firm is real.

**Differentiator:** partners are former C-suite operators, and the firm has a
published record of real engagements with named sectors, regions and deal sizes.
Not technology.

**What the site must do:** say what the firm does and for whom above the fold,
show the record, make the partners credible, and open a conversation.

**Constraints:** DESIGN.md is the single source for all visual tokens. British
English. WCAG 2.2 AA. No AI or stock photography of people. No claim about
countries, deal value, team size or track record ships without a confirmed
source — unconfirmed figures appear as TKTK.
